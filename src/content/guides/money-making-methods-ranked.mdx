These SkyBlock money making methods ranked by coins per hour are fifteen of the methods on the SkyCofl tasks list, sorted by what you need to start. If you have under 1M coins and no skill above XI, start with Flint Mining. If you have Catacombs 36 or an Infernal-ready Kuudra party, M7 and Kuudra Infernal are the highest-paying methods here that do not consume a costly bait.

## Where do the coins/hour figures come from?

The tasks list ranks methods by what SkyCofl mod users actually collected while doing them, priced at current bazaar and auction values. It counts drops only: keys, bait, paid chests and gear wear are not subtracted, and your own rate depends on your gear, skills and luck.

The method pages read a few minutes later showed small drifts from the list (Lunar Moth Trapping 52.2M, Mithril Mining 2.7M). The table below uses the list figures.

## What should you do with little capital?

**Flint Mining (20.4M/hour).** Craft a Flint Shovel from 10 Flint and 2 Sticks once you reach Flint Collection II; it guarantees Flint from every Gravel block in the Spider's Den Gravel Mines. A [Flint Shovel](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/FLINT_SHOVEL) was also listed for 185 coins on the auction house on November 16 (15:09 UTC), so the entry cost is close to nothing.

Nearly all of the rate is one item: [Enchanted Flint](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_FLINT) at 11.6k per hour and 1.7k each makes 20.1M of the 20.4M. Its bazaar price fell 25.5% over the month, from 2.5k on October 18 to 1.9k on November 15. Every further drop cuts your hourly rate by the same share, and below about 500 coins per Enchanted Flint (11.6k per hour plus the roughly 0.3M of other drops), Flint would pay less than Pest.

**Pest (6.1M/hour) and Garden Visitors (2.7M/hour).** Both need SkyBlock level 5 and a Garden. For Pest you claim a SkyMart Vacuum from Pesthunter Phillip at Garden level V and kill pests on your plots with it. Their largest listed drop is SkyBlock Copper (818.2k/hour for Pest, 896.2k/hour for visitors); visitors also drop [Jacob's Tickets](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/JACOBS_TICKET) at 16.0k each.

**Water Fishing (4.4M/hour) and Revenant Slayer (3.0M/hour).** Neither lists a requirement. Water Fishing's largest drop is [Ink Sac](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/INK_SACK) at 1.3M/hour, and Ink Sac fell 13.0% over the month (2.9k to 2.5k). Revenant Slayer's largest is [Foul Flesh](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/FOUL_FLESH) at 823.5k/hour, priced at 24.0k each.

## Which mid-game methods pay best once you have skills?

**Cactus Farming (30.0M/hour) and Wheat Farming (19.0M/hour).** The tasks page only asks for SkyBlock level 5 and names the Cactus Knife and Euclid's Wheat Hoe as the tools for Farming Fortune. Neither tool had an auction quote on November 16, so budget them from the [farming progression guide](/guides/farming-progression-guide-2026). The largest single drops are [Enchanted Cactus Green](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_CACTUS_GREEN) (8.8M/hour at 587 each) and [Enchanted Wheat](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_WHEAT) (6.3M/hour at 928 each).

**Jade Mining (25.4M/hour).** You need Mining 12 and a tool with Breaking Power 7 or higher; the tasks page does not name a specific tool, so this guide gives no price for one. The requirement list says Heart of the Mountain 3, but the same page's directions say entering the Crystal Hollows takes HotM tier 4 and a Crystal Hollows Pass, so plan for tier 4. [Fine Jade Gemstone](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/FINE_JADE_GEM) carries 19.7M of the rate at 24.4k each; it slipped 6.0% over the month.

**Ghost in The Mist (17.4M/hour).** You need Mining 12 and a melee weapon, because Ghosts are immune to bows, magic and abilities. The cheapest weapon the page names, an [Emerald Blade](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/EMERALD_BLADE), was 368.6k; a [Giant's Sword](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/GIANTS_SWORD) was 60.00M and a [Dark Claymore](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/DARK_CLAYMORE) 158.00M. [Plasma](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/PLASMA) (10.1M/hour at 298.0k) rose 89.7% over the month, from 206.9k to 392.4k, so this rank depends on a price that almost doubled in four weeks.

**Blaze Slayer (18.2M/hour).** You need Combat 22 and a Voidgloom Seraph tier 3 kill, plus paired Blaze Slayer daggers from tier II. The tasks page does not name which daggers; the wiki lists the [Firedust Dagger](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/FIREDUST_DAGGER) and [Twilight Dagger](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/MAWDUST_DAGGER) among Blaze Slayer drops, each 2.50M on the auction house on November 17, 2026. [Derelict Ashe](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/DERELICT_ASHE) is the largest drop at 7.0M/hour; it fell 20.3% over the month, from 1.9k to 1.5k.

**Experimentation Table (12.0M/hour).** The table recipe unlocks at Enchanting X, and the tasks page marks the method as Limited. Charges come back 24 hours after the last one is used, and extra charges cost Bits. [Titanic Experience Bottles](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/TITANIC_EXP_BOTTLE) at 525.6k each are 5.4M of the rate.

**Mithril Mining (2.6M/hour).** It needs Mining 12 and a pickaxe with Breaking Power 4 to enter the Dwarven Mines, and it pays the least of the methods here. [Enchanted Mithril](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_MITHRIL), its largest drop at 824.6k/hour, sold at 1.2k.

## What do the late-game methods cost to start?

**Kuudra T5 (41.7M/hour).** You need Combat 22, a Fiery clear and 12,000 reputation with a Crimson Isle faction, plus a party of four. The page names a [Hyperion](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/HYPERION) (502.50M on the auction house) with an [Aurora Chestplate](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/AURORA_CHESTPLATE) (2.00M). The rate counts drops only; each paid chest also takes an Infernal key, about 3.22M coins on September 26 per the [Kuudra profit guide](/guides/kuudra-profit-guide-2026), and the largest listed drops are [Vampiric Vitality V](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTMENT_MANA_VAMPIRE_5) books (5.6M/hour at 466.1k) and [Heavy Pearl](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/HEAVY_PEARL) (5.1M/hour at 287.3k).

**M7 (37.1M/hour).** You need Combat 15, Catacombs 36 and a Master Floor 6 clear, with a party of five. [Wither Essence](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_WITHER) at 2.9k is the steady drop, and a [Necron's Handle](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/NECRON_HANDLE) at 431.20M is the rare one, at a base chance of about 1 in 772 Bedrock chests. Paid chests cost coins on top, and Wither Essence alone comes to 1.1M/hour on the method page, so a session without a rare drop earns far less than the average.

**Lunar Moth Trapping (52.3M/hour).** The only listed requirement is SkyBlock level 5, but every moth costs one Moondew, so this is a capital method. The worked example below shows why its rank does not account for the bait bill.

## Worked example: does Lunar Moth Trapping pay?

You place a Pest, Mouse or Vermin Trap in your Garden and bait it with [Moondew](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/MOONDEW). A [Pest Trap](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/PEST_TRAP) costs 50 Pests from Pesthunter Phillip per the community wiki, or 300.0k on the auction house on November 17, 2026. Per the wiki, Moondew guarantees a Lunar Moth, and every pest that spawns in a trap consumes one bait.

Each moth drops a [Sunset I book](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTMENT_ULTIMATE_SUNSET_1) at a 35% base chance. Since May 14, 2026 that chance scales with Overbloom, so your real rate depends on your setup. Lassoing a moth gives [Lunar Moth Shards](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/SHARD_LUNAR_MOTH), in amounts set by Hunting Fortune.

| Per moth (November 16, 2026) | Figure |
| --- | ---: |
| Moondew crafted with buy orders | 659.1k |
| Moondew bought instantly | 1.02M |
| Sunset I book, sold instantly | 592.2k |
| Book value at the 35% base chance (0.35 × 592.2k) | about 207.3k |
| Gap if every moth dropped a book (659.1k − 592.2k) | 66.9k |

Even a book from every moth would not cover the cheapest Moondew; the 66.9k gap has to come from the moth's crops and shards. The tasks page gives those per hour, not per moth, so it cannot show whether tracked players actually come out ahead. Even your own crafted Moondew is worth 722.0k sold instantly, so each moth has to return more than that to beat simply selling the bait.

Moondew rose 78.6% over the month, from 719.4k on October 18 to 1.28M on November 15, and its lowest point in that window was 362.1k. Sunset I fell 20.5% over the same month, from 756.7k to 601.4k, so the bait got dearer while the book it chases got cheaper.

## Where do these rankings go wrong?

- **Costs are missing.** Keys, Moondew, paid chests and Bits are not subtracted, which is how Lunar Moth Trapping ranks first although its bait costs more than its main drop.
- **One drop carries the rate.** Flint, Jade and Lunar Moth each earn most of their figure from a single item, so one price move resets the ranking.
- **Rare drops inflate averages.** M7's rate includes Necron's Handles you may not see for many sessions.
- **Some figures rest on few hours.** Lunar Moth Trapping's figure comes from 18 tracked hours by 21 contributors, so a few sessions can move it.

## When does each method stop being worth it?

Drop a method when its main item falls far enough that the next method on your stage pays more. Flint stops leading early game when Enchanted Flint falls below about 500 coins; Ghost loses its place if Plasma returns to its October 18 level of 206.9k. Lunar Moth Trapping only pays if one moth's book, crops and shards are worth more than one Moondew, which a Sunset I book alone did not reach on November 16.

Check the [live tasks list](https://sky.coflnet.com/tasks) before each long session rather than this snapshot. Running `/cofl task` in game with the SkyCofl mod gives a figure based on your own stats instead of the community average.
