## August economy scoreboard

| Method or market | August direction | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Torrhus progression goods | Gained attention | 0.27 added Helix Log, Honeycomb and Ruby Veilshroom collections plus roughly 150 new Attributes |
| Torrhus fishing | Gained output, mixed item outlook | Veilshroom base yield rose from 1 to 4–8, while one important recipe fell from 16 Veilshrooms to 2 |
| Critter Safari | Gained progression value | Milestones became faster on August 11, then Safari became the intended Hunting XP route on August 19 |
| Mangrove chopping | Stronger NPC floor | Mangrove Log NPC sell value moved from 8 to 15 coins in 0.27 |

The table describes patch direction, not observed live prices. Its mechanics come from the current wiki changelogs for [0.27 on August 4](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_4), the [August 11 Torrhus follow-up](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_11/0), the [August 19 Safari rebalance](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_19), and the [August 20 Greenhouse patch](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_20).

## The headline move: 0.27 created a new progression economy

The August 4 release added Torrhus Canyon and Critter Safari together. Torrhus introduced three collections—Helix Log, Ruby Veilshroom and Honeycomb—while the two islands added roughly 150 Attributes, approximately doubling the total Attribute pool. Safari Tickets connect Miria's contests to the Critter Safari, where caught critters award Attribute Shards. Those are direct new uses for player time and new inputs for progression, crafting and fusion, not a cosmetic side market. The [current 0.27 changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_4) documents the collections, tickets, critter rewards and Attribute expansion.

That explains the month's broad rotation: established methods did not all become worse, but every hour spent on them now competes with fresh collection unlocks, Safari milestones and shard discovery. Fiyrthgu's [August 7 Torrhus money-making test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FhMIbK75ZPE) and BarInTokyo's [August 10 Torrhus fishing test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPtQbAlCZGw) show creators testing that new opportunity set during launch week. Neither video is a safe current price sheet, so this recap uses them only as evidence of the methods being tested, not as a rate guarantee.

## What gained ground

### Helix progression and the wider Galatea loop

Helix Trees are the source of Helix Logs and Desert Whispers, and Desert Whispers upgrade Heart of the Forest tiers 4–8. The same launch patch changed several Sweep percentages into flat Sweep, fixed the thrown-axe penalty, lowered every tree's later Gift milestones, and made a Jade Dragon Egg require 64 Helixis alongside reduced Figstone and Mangcore inputs. Those changes give Helix chopping a progression role while also renewing demand across older Galatea currencies. See the [August 4 changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_4) and the existing [post-Torrhus Sweep guide](/guides/post-torrhus-canyon-update) for the complete upgrade order.

Mangrove chopping also received a clearer floor: its NPC sell value increased from 8 to 15 coins per log while Fig remained at 7. That does not establish a best coins/hour method, but it makes Mangrove output less dependent on finding a player-market buyer. Treat NPC value as a floor and compare it with the current Bazaar route before selling; the value change is listed in the [0.27 changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_4).

### Torrhus fishing—better output, but not a simple stockpile

On August 11, base Ruby Veilshroom yield increased from 1 to 4–8, rarer sea creatures gained a chance to drop Veilshroom Bunches directly, and overall Veilshroom gain from Torrhus sea creatures increased. In isolation, that is a substantial output buff for the fishing loop documented in BarInTokyo's [Torrhus fishing video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPtQbAlCZGw).

Supply was not the only side of the patch. The Accretion Artifact recipe simultaneously fell from 16 Ruby Veilshrooms to 2. The safe conclusion is therefore narrower than “fishing up means Veilshrooms up”: the method yields more, while one named ingredient sink needs far fewer units. The [August 11 changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_11/0) supports both changes. Sell in measured lots until live volume shows whether other uses absorb the extra supply.

### Critter Safari as the intended Hunting route

The first follow-up patch reduced Safari Milestone V–X requirements, with the final milestone falling from 1,500 catches to 750. It also shortened the Birdfeeder's maximum wait from 30 seconds to 10 and adjusted several critter spawn counts. Those changes made a Safari run less idle and moved its progression rewards closer; exact changes are in the [August 11 notes](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_11/0).

August 19 made the direction explicit. Each unique critter now awards 5,000 Hunting XP, for up to 185,000 before other factors; floor-drop Hunting XP increased to 2,500–10,000, and charming XP increased tenfold. At the same time, Fusion Machine Hunting XP fell by 75% and Hunting XP from Ghosts and Glacite Walkers fell by 50%. The stated goal was to make Critter Safari the optimal Hunting XP route. These are progression rates, not coin rates, but they matter economically because they redirect players toward Safari rewards and away from grinding fusion or combat primarily for Hunting levels. See the [August 19 changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_19).

## What lost ground

### Passive Greenhouse income no longer deserves July assumptions

The clearest loser is the “set and forget” Greenhouse routine described in the [July farming trend report](/guides/hypixel-skyblock-farming-trends-july-2026). On August 20, the developers said mutations had produced 10.17% of all generated coins over the previous seven days despite low effort. They changed most mutation multipliers and gave base crops a 72-hour decay timer specifically to require periodic replanting. The [current Greenhouse changelog](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_20) is unusually direct about both the economic reason and the intended behavior change.

This was a rebalance, not an equal nerf to every mutation. Ashwreath's multiplier fell from 0.3 to 0.15 and Witherbloom's from 0.375 to 0.23, while Snoozling rose from 4 to 21, Glasscorn from 8 to 16 and Stoplight Petal from 8 to 25. A lower multiplier means less loot from that mutation. The same [August 20 table](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_20) supports each figure.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Do not copy a pre-August Greenhouse layout blindly.">
The correct response is to audit your mutation mix and maintenance schedule, not abandon the Greenhouse automatically. Some outputs were buffed sharply, but the passive profile changed because base crops now decay after 72 hours.
</Callout>

### Fusion as an XP shortcut

Shard fusion still has its core economic purpose, and the larger Attribute pool gives traders more combinations to evaluate. What lost ground is using the Fusion Machine mainly as a Hunting-level shortcut: its Hunting XP reward was cut by 75% on August 19. An August 11 fix also made Special Fusions appear before ID Fusions and prevented ID-fusing directly into Power Dragon, preserving its intended M7 obtainment path. The mechanics are documented in the wiki's [August 19](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_19) and [second August 11](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Changelog/2026/August_11/1) changelogs.

For a trader, that means value the output path first and XP second. Use a live fusion scanner, include failed or alternate outcomes in the risk check, and do not price a route as though every previously possible ID outcome remains available.

## What to do differently than last month

| July habit | August adjustment | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leave one passive Greenhouse layout indefinitely | Recalculate mutation mix and plan to replant within the 72-hour base-crop window | Multipliers changed and permanent base crops were removed |
| Treat Farming as the only major low-attention rotation | Test one Torrhus loop with the progression you already own | 0.27 added three collections, Safari milestones and a much larger Attribute pool |
| Judge Torrhus fishing by launch-day output | Measure post-August 11 Veilshroom yield and current sell-through together | Output rose while the Accretion Artifact sink fell from 16 units to 2 |
| Use fusion or combat chiefly to level Hunting | Move Hunting progression sessions into Critter Safari | Safari XP rose while Fusion Machine, Ghost and Glacite Walker XP fell |
| Buy every new shard or Sweep upgrade immediately | Finish free milestones, then compare cost per applicable upgrade | August 11 reduced later Safari requirements and 0.27 reduced Tree Gift thresholds |

The practical routine is simple: benchmark your existing method for one session, benchmark one Torrhus alternative for the same amount of active time, and compare realized sales rather than inventory valuation. For tradeable new goods, check margin, volume and fill speed using the process in the [market literacy guide](/guides/market-literacy-margin-volume-risk). For Helix progression, follow the [August 11 Sweep guide](/guides/post-torrhus-canyon-update) before buying the luxury tier.

## Bottom line

August rewards active re-testing. Torrhus foraging, fishing and Safari progression gained new or improved reward loops, while the passive Greenhouse economy was deliberately pulled back and made more maintenance-heavy. The best move is not a universal switch into one method: keep any stable July routine that still performs on your profile, but stop valuing it with July inputs. Test the post-patch version, sell new-update goods only where real volume exists, and put progression coins into the cheapest upgrade that improves the method you actually use.
