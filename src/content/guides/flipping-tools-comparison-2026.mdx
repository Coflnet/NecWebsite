## Do I need a guide or a scanner?

Use a guide when you cannot yet explain why a candidate should sell, what the fees do to the exit, or how long you can hold the inventory. Use a scanner or market page as a source of candidates to investigate once you can perform those checks yourself. Reject any candidate whose apparent profit relies on an unsupported sale price.

For a worked price check, the [29 October Bazaar example](/guides/bazaar-flipping-guide-2026) shows why a positive quoted spread can still produce a loss on an immediate round trip. It is quote arithmetic, not a recommendation to buy that item.
