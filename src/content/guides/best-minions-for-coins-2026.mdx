## Why does Fishing win while you are offline?

[Fishing Minions](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Fishing_Minion) only perform harvest actions, so they do not spend alternating actions placing and breaking a resource or spawning and killing a mob. Their seven-drop output also prices much better at the Bazaar than at NPCs: the wiki calculator put Tier XI at 169,837 coins per day with an Enchanted Lava Bucket, but only 29,640 coins per day at NPC value on August 24, 2026.

Melon looks stronger in the same calculator. On August 24, 2026, Tier XII reached 283,536 coins per day with the high-budget modifiers above, but the current [Melon Minion wiki page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Melon_Minion) documents that offline calculations generate melons twice as slowly because they assume an extra replant action. That makes Melon an island-loaded option, not the safer answer to unattended coins per day.

## What does each budget actually buy?

The low-budget build uses the cumulative Fishing V recipe: 960 [Raw Cod](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/RAW_FISH) at 168.3 coins each and eight [Enchanted Raw Cod](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_RAW_FISH) at 1,195.7 coins each. Add a [Super Compactor 3000](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/SUPER_COMPACTOR_3000) at 148,000 coins, an [Enchanted Lava Bucket](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_LAVA_BUCKET) at 65,700 coins, and a Small Storage crafted from 64 Oak Logs, for about 385,000 coins per slot as of August 24, 2026.

Fishing XI needs eight [Enchanted Cooked Cod](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_COOKED_FISH) at 150,100 coins each, 992 Enchanted Raw Cod, and 960 Raw Cod cumulatively. With the same compactor and fuel plus a [Large Storage](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/LARGE_ENCHANTED_CHEST) at 806,300 coins, the full slot costs about 3.57M coins as of August 24, 2026.

The high build adds a [Fishing Minion XII Upgrade Stone](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/GENERATOR_UPGRADE_STONE_FISHING_12) at 10.38M coins, a [Plasma Bucket](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/PLASMA_BUCKET) at 11.00M, one [Flycatcher](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/FLYCATCHER_UPGRADE) at 25.38M, and a [Mithril Infusion](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/MITHRIL_INFUSION) at 4.31M. Those August 24, 2026 instant-buy prices put the whole slot near 54.57M coins; it earns the most of the three builds, but not the best return on each coin spent.

## What does a real Bazaar order change?

Take an order of 1,000 Raw Cod on August 24, 2026. The live buy price was 168.3 coins and the sell price was 96.1, so a buy order at the sell side followed by a sell offer at the buy side would cost 96,100 coins and return 168,300 coins: a **72,200-coin gross margin before Bazaar fees**, if both orders filled at those prices.

That spread was unusually wide, so it is an exit example rather than a promised flip. On August 24, 2026, the same figures showed why an instant sale could give up 72.2 coins per Raw Cod compared with a filled sell offer.

## How long does the Tier XII upgrade take to repay?

Keep the high-budget modifiers fixed and compare Fishing XI with Fishing XII. On August 24, 2026, the wiki calculator gave 225,697 coins per day at Tier XI and 253,669 at Tier XII, so the upgrade added 27,972 coins per day.

At the August 24, 2026 instant-buy price of 10.38M coins, the Upgrade Stone takes about **371 days** to repay: `10,380,000 / 27,972 = 371`. A buy order at the observed 8.40M sell-side price reduces that to about 300 days, but either result assumes output prices and minion mechanics stay unchanged for most of a year.

That is why Tier XI is the practical stopping point. Upgrade to XII for minion completion, slot optimization or a long holding period, not because “maxed” automatically means good ROI.

## Which fuel and upgrades should you buy first?

[Minion Fuel](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Minion_Fuel) increases speed, and permanent fuels can be removed and reused. As of August 24, 2026, the 65,700-coin Enchanted Lava Bucket gives +25% speed indefinitely while the 11.00M Plasma Bucket gives +35%, so the cheap bucket belongs in every low- and mid-budget slot before the marginal ten percentage points become worth considering.

A minion has only two [upgrade slots](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Minion_Upgrades). Fishing normally uses a Super Compactor 3000 first, because uncompressed multi-item output consumes storage quickly, then a Flycatcher only in the expensive build; using two Flycatchers would remove the compactor and turn collection frequency into the limiting factor.

Mithril Infusion is a separate permanent modifier, so it does not consume either upgrade slot. It costs 4.31M coins for +10% speed as of August 24, 2026, which makes it another late purchase rather than a starter upgrade.

## How much storage do you need?

Storage does not increase the production rate; it protects the rate you already paid for. Fishing V has 704 units, or 11 item slots, of internal capacity, but the calculator's August 24, 2026 output occupies 13 stacks over 24 hours; the wiki's [Small Storage](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Minion_Storage) adds three slots and is the smallest sufficient buffer. Fishing XI has 960 units internally, while Large Storage adds 15 slots.

Use Small Storage with the low-budget build, then add Large Storage when you leave the island for longer intervals. If a minion still fills between collections, storage or compaction is the next purchase even when a speed upgrade looks better on paper, because a full minion stops preserving Bazaar-priced output.

## Should the output go to NPC or Bazaar?

Fishing is a Bazaar choice. Check the [live minion calculator](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Calculator:MinionFullProfitTable) before you buy more slots, then sell the compacted drops through the Bazaar route shown as more valuable; on August 24, 2026, the Tier XI comparison was 169,837 coins at Bazaar value versus 29,640 at NPC value.

As of August 24, 2026, an [Enchanted Hopper](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Enchanted_Hopper) only sells overfill at 70% of NPC value. It is insurance when you cannot collect, not a substitute for storage on a Bazaar minion, and the [live item page](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ENCHANTED_HOPPER) priced one at 319,400 coins on that date.

NPC-focused minions trade upside for predictability. The wiki calculator put Snow XI at 24,945 coins per day and Clay XI at 32,400 with no fuel on August 24, 2026, so both remain useful for collection or skill experience but are weak purchases when coins per day is the goal.

## Which popular minions are worse than their reputation?

**Slime is no longer the automatic passive-money answer.** The current [Slime Minion page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Slime_Minion) still recommends Corrupt Soil, but the economics around the familiar hopper setup changed: Corrupted Fragments fell from 20 NPC coins to 1 on January 28, 2025, and Enchanted Hoppers returned 70% of NPC value as of August 24, 2026.

Using the wiki's Slime XI 12-second action time, an Enchanted Lava Bucket produced about 4,500 kills per day on August 24, 2026. Offline, two Slimeballs plus one Sulphur and one Corrupted Fragment are worth 21 NPC coins per kill, so the 70% hopper rate on August 24, 2026 returned about **66,150 coins per day before Diamond Spreading**; the wiki documents an island-loaded bug that could raise that loaded figure to about 100,800, but neither case makes Tier XI Slime the better unattended buy.

**Snow and Clay are stable, not competitive.** Their fixed NPC exits and mining or fishing experience can still justify keeping them, but their current daily coin output does not support buying them as your next profit upgrade.

**Inferno is a fuel bet, not a clean high-budget tier.** [Inferno Minions](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Inferno_Minion) are extremely slow without their exclusive 24-hour fuel, and Hypergolic fuel adds rare-drop variance; compare the full daily fuel cost with the value actually collected instead of treating an Apex drop as guaranteed income.

## When does this recommendation stop working?

Recalculate when Fishing output prices fall, Raw Cod upgrade materials rise, Hypixel changes offline simulation, or the Enchanted Hopper and Corrupted Fragment NPC values change again. A new minion or a fix to Melon's documented offline behavior could also move the unattended ranking immediately.

For now, buy breadth first, Fishing V second, and Fishing XI third. The high-budget build is the highest daily figure here, but its Tier XII stone alone needs roughly a year to repay, so most players should spend those coins on more productive slots or an active money method instead.
