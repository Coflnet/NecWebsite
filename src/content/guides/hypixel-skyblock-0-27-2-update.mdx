## What do you need before buying upgrades?

For the bait-sack route, you need the Raw Cod collection unlock: the walkthroughs show the small, medium and large sacks at collection levels 3, 5 and 7 respectively. For the drone route, you need a Precursor Drone and access to the [Forest Essence](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_FOREST) shop; buying the upgrade is only one part of that setup. If you cannot yet reach the shop or use the pet, keep your coins for progression instead.

The Dungeon potion accessory is relevant when you already run Catacombs floors and use Dungeon potions. Treat it as a convenience purchase, with an ongoing potion budget, rather than a reason to start spending on Dungeons.

These mechanics are shown in [Udex2’s Minister update walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ljxp9yJeZBk) and [2-B-Determined Gaming’s item walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDD1ZToeuXU), as checked on October 7, 2026. Their auto-generated captions support the changes below; this is a focused set of practical upgrades rather than an exhaustive changelog.

## Which Minister changes can you use immediately?

**Choose bait inside your sack.** Both walkthroughs show bait moving into the new sacks, with shift-left-click used to select the bait you want to fish with. Put the sack in your Sack of Sacks and check the selected bait before your next session, especially after moving supplies out of the old fishing bag.

**Specialize your Precursor Drone.** Both videos show drone upgrades from the Forest Essence and Diamond Essence shops, alongside a fishing-related upgrade made from Backwater Bayou materials. As shown on October 7, the Forest Essence upgrade costs 2,500 essence and gives 50 Foraging Fortune; start by pricing that upgrade against the activity you actually play.

**Check automatic potion spending.** Both walkthroughs show a Dungeon accessory that consumes your selected Dungeon potion and can buy one if you do not have it. Review its selection and automatic-purchase setting before a run, so convenience does not become an unnoticed recurring expense.

## What does the update mean for your spending?

A new use for an item is a reason to check its live price, not proof that its price will rise. For the drone upgrade, open [Forest Essence on SkyCofl](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_FOREST) before buying the full amount.

At **06:42 UTC on October 7, 2026**, Forest Essence has a **329-coin buy quote** and a **301.5-coin sell quote**, a **27.5-coin spread per essence**. The shop upgrade shown in both videos needs 2,500 essence, so its essence-only purchase budget is **2,500 × 329 = 822,500 coins** at that quote.

For comparison, selling that same 2,500 essence at the quoted sell price returns **753,750 coins before fees**. The difference is **68,750 coins before fees**, so buying the ingredients and changing your mind has a measurable cost even if the quotes stay unchanged; this is a purchase-cost example, not a profitable flip.

That budget excludes the pet and any other upgrade. It also assumes the displayed quote is available for the whole quantity: check the order depth and final total before confirming your purchase.

## What should you do this week?

1. Check your fishing collection and choose the bait sack you can already craft. Verify the selected bait before fishing.
2. If you already use a Precursor Drone for foraging, compare the essence-only budget with your available coins before buying its Forest Essence upgrade.
3. If you want automatic Dungeon potions, check which potion is selected and whether automatic purchases are enabled before you begin a session.

Avoid buying a whole set of upgrades just because they arrived together. Pick one that removes a problem in your current routine, then judge whether the improvement justifies the expense.

## When should you recheck this advice?

Recalculate the essence budget if the buy quote, available depth or shop cost changes. A later patch to drone bonuses, bait selection or automatic potion purchases would also change the relevant recommendation; verify the current in-game tooltip before spending.

The October 7 quote is a dated comparison, not a price target. Use the live item page again when you are ready to buy.
