## What do you need before buying upgrades?

For the bait-sack route, you need the Raw Cod collection unlock: the walkthroughs show the small, medium and large sacks at collection levels 3, 5 and 7 respectively. For the drone route, you need a Precursor Drone and access to the [Forest Essence](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_FOREST) shop; buying the upgrade is only one part of that setup. If you cannot yet reach the shop or use the pet, keep your coins for progression instead.

The InfiniPot accessory is sold by Malik for 5,000 [Wither Essence](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_WITHER) and is relevant when you already run Catacombs floors and use Dungeon potions. Treat it as a convenience purchase, with an ongoing potion budget, rather than a reason to start spending on Dungeons.

These mechanics are shown in [Udex2’s Minister update walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ljxp9yJeZBk) and [2-B-Determined Gaming’s item walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WDD1ZToeuXU), as checked on October 7, 2026. Their auto-generated captions support the collection levels and Foraging Fortune bonus below. The [official 0.27.2 release thread](https://hypixel.net/threads/hypixel-skyblock-0-27-2-the-minister-update-greenhouse-qol-and-more.6159904/), checked on October 9, 2026, establishes which perks changed in this release and supplies the other changes below; this remains a focused set of practical upgrades rather than an exhaustive changelog.

## Which Minister changes can you use immediately?

**Choose bait inside your sack.** Both walkthroughs show bait moving into the new sacks, with shift-left-click used to select the bait you want to fish with. Put the sack in your Sack of Sacks and check the selected bait before your next session, especially after moving supplies out of the old fishing bag.

**Specialize your Precursor Drone.** The update makes the drone base Legendary, with three swappable Drone Mods: Grungle from the Forest Essence shop, Mining off Camera from the Diamond Essence shop for 2,500 [Diamond Essence](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_DIAMOND), and Contraband crafted from Backwater Bayou junk. As shown on October 7, the Forest Essence upgrade costs 2,500 essence and gives 50 Foraging Fortune; start by pricing that upgrade against the activity you actually play.

**Check automatic potion spending.** The InfiniPot consumes a Dungeon potion from your inventory or potion bag when a run begins. If you have none, it buys your desired tier using coins from your purse or bank. Review its selection and automatic-purchase setting before a run, so convenience does not become an unnoticed recurring expense.

## Which Minister perks were already available?

**Potato Ring and Blood God Sigil were already in the game and unchanged in 0.27.2.** The release thread includes them in the complete Minister perk list, but explicitly says they were not adjusted in this release. They upgrade the Potato Talisman and Blood God Talisman using items gathered from Shiny Pigs.

The unchanged perk does not mean every Year of the Pig reward is unchanged. Under general changes, the 0.27.2 thread adds a pity for the [Blood God Crest](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/BLOOD_GOD_CREST) at **1,000 Shiny Pigs** and an **NPC sell price of 500,000 coins**; these are release values, not a live market quote. It also adds a Potato Talisman pity at 2,000 Shiny Pigs.

**Kuudra’s Kidney, Lung and Heart were also already in the game and unchanged in 0.27.2.** The thread describes this accessory line as crafted from Kuudra Tentacles bought from paid Kuudra chests. Do not treat either accessory group as a newly introduced reason to buy ingredients for this patch.

## What else changes your routine in 0.27.2?

**Keep Greenhouse mutations without timing every login.** A mutation now needs both its decay timer and its minimum mutation value to reach zero before it decays; an expired timer extends by 24 hours while that minimum remains unmet. Unwatered plants halt instead of dying, but cannot spread mutations, share crop effects or count toward the unique bonus until watered.

The maximum Unique Crop Bonus now needs 10 unique crops instead of 12, but its maximum Growth Speed and Yield bonuses both fall to 25%, from 30% and 36% respectively. Recheck a layout that relied on those old bonuses; the Plant Diagnostics Tool’s new Sanity Check also shows which mutations can grow in a spot.

**Plan mining around Cole’s whole term.** When Cole has the Mining Fiesta perk, the fiesta now spans his entire mayorship, with its base Mining Fortune buff reduced to +25%. Brynmor sells up to five Fiesta Flasks that stack additively with that buff, so choose when to use them around the sessions you can actually play.

**Check your Dungeon party’s completions.** Boss and Watcher dialogue, cutscenes and Watcher summons are faster, with most time saves conditional on every player having cleared the floor at least once. Revive Stones now work in solo Dungeons, while Spirit Masks gain a Master Mode Floor IV requirement; check that requirement before relying on one.

**Preview reforges before applying them.** Right-click a Reforge Stone to inspect its stats at every rarity, or open `/reforges` through chat, the Hex or the Blacksmith. Advanced Reforge application costs now depend on the stone’s rarity instead of the target item’s rarity, so recheck the displayed fee before confirming.

**Recheck combat rewards and returning-player help.** Mob levels now generally derive from health and damage, with those levels feeding into skill XP, coin and XP-orb rewards. If you have been offline for at least 90 days, visit Calvin on your Private Island for returning-player buffs lasting seven days.

## What does the update mean for your spending?

A new use for an item is a reason to check its live price, not proof that its price will rise. For the drone upgrade, open [Forest Essence on SkyCofl](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_FOREST) before buying the full amount.

At **22:01 UTC on October 9, 2026**, Forest Essence has a **373-coin buy quote** and a **304.9-coin sell quote**, a **68.1-coin spread per essence**. The shop upgrade shown in both videos needs 2,500 essence, so its essence-only purchase budget is **2,500 × 373 = 932,500 coins** at that quote.

For comparison, selling that same 2,500 essence at the quoted sell price returns **762,250 coins before fees**. The difference is **170,250 coins before fees**, so buying the ingredients and changing your mind has a measurable cost even if the quotes stay unchanged; this is a purchase-cost example, not a profitable flip.

At **22:02 UTC on October 9, 2026**, Diamond Essence has a **347.2-coin buy quote**, putting Mining off Camera’s 2,500-essence cost at **868,000 coins**. Wither Essence is quoted at **3.4k coins to buy** (rounded), so InfiniPot’s 5,000-essence shop price needs **roughly 17 million coins** if you buy all the essence. Check the live total before choosing that convenience purchase; Dungeon potions remain an additional expense.

These essence budgets exclude the pet, other upgrades and ongoing potion purchases. They also assume the displayed quote is available for the whole quantity: check the order depth and final total before confirming your purchase.

## How have prices changed since the earlier snapshot?

At **06:42 UTC on October 7, 2026**, Forest Essence’s buy quote was **329 coins** and its sell quote was **301.5 coins**. Against the October 9 quote above, buying 2,500 essence now costs **110,000 coins more**: **932,500 instead of 822,500**, an increase of **13.4%**. Its quoted spread has also widened from **27.5 to 68.1 coins per essence**, so reversing the purchase is more expensive before fees.

The broader [Forest Essence price history](https://sky.coflnet.com/item/ESSENCE_FOREST) moves in the other direction: the monthly series falls from **444.1 coins at 02:00 UTC on September 10, 2026** to **373.4 coins at 02:00 UTC on October 8**, a **15.9% decline**. That series and the October 7–9 buy quotes cover different windows; the recent increase does not erase the longer decline.

These are dated market comparisons, not a measurement from the release instant or proof that the Minister update caused either move. For your purchase, the practical change is the higher essence budget since October 7: reprice the full 2,500 before committing, even if an older guide or monthly chart suggests a cheaper upgrade.

## What should you do this week?

1. Check your fishing collection and choose the bait sack you can already craft. Verify the selected bait before fishing.
2. If you already use a Precursor Drone for foraging, compare the essence-only budget with your available coins before buying its Forest Essence upgrade.
3. If you want automatic Dungeon potions, check which potion is selected and whether automatic purchases are enabled before you begin a session.

Avoid buying a whole set of upgrades just because they arrived together. Pick one that removes a problem in your current routine, then judge whether the improvement justifies the expense.

## When should you recheck this advice?

Recalculate the essence budget if the buy quote, available depth or shop cost changes. A later patch to drone bonuses, bait selection or automatic potion purchases would also change the relevant recommendation; verify the current in-game tooltip before spending.

The October 9 quote is a dated comparison, not a price target. Use the live item page again when you are ready to buy.
