Dungeon profit depends on your clear time, score, chest roll and current resale proceeds. Compare the chest price with a current Bazaar or Auction House exit before you buy, and use completed-run data instead of assuming that a newly unlocked floor pays more.

<Callout title="The short answer:">
The current wiki supports floor unlocks, score rules and loot tables, but it does not publish a profitability ranking by Catacombs level. Compare only floors you have unlocked, and keep enough coins for their largest target chest: normal Floor VII reaches 100M for Necron's Handle, while Master Mode VII reaches 150M for Dark Claymore.
</Callout>

## What do you need before the coin figures matter?

You need **Combat XV** to enter the Catacombs. You must also have completed the previous floor and meet the Catacombs level for the floor you want to enter; the [current Dungeons requirements table](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeons#Dungeons) lists every normal and Master Mode gate.

| Catacombs level | Newly available normal floor | Newly available Master Mode floor |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Floor I | — |
| 3 | Floor II | — |
| 5 | Floor III | — |
| 9 | Floor IV | — |
| 14 | Floor V | — |
| 19 | Floor VI | — |
| 24 | Floor VII | Master Mode I, after a Floor VII completion |
| 26 / 28 / 30 | — | Master Mode II / III / IV |
| 32 / 34 / 36 | — | Master Mode V / VI / VII |

There is no separate class-level minimum in that requirements table. You can enter as Healer, Mage, Berserk, Archer or Tank, but you must reach **Class Milestone 2 during the run** to claim a reward chest. Choose the class that lets you reach that milestone early without slowing the clear; a class that survives but misses its milestone earns no chest profit.

## Which unlocked floor pays best for your account?

The current wiki does not provide coins-per-hour results for each floor, so it cannot support a universal claim that Floor IV, V, VI, VII or a Master Mode floor is the profitable choice at a given level. Catacombs level only determines which floors join your shortlist.

Test the newly unlocked floor against the floor you already complete reliably. For ten completed runs on each, record score, duration, chest bought, chest cost and actual sale proceeds; include failed runs in the total time. The floor with the higher **net coins per minute** is the better money floor for your account until loot prices or your clear speed change.

## How much coin reserve does each floor need?

The figures below are **chest purchase prices, not resale values**, checked in the wiki's current floor loot tables on August 23, 2026. Paid chest bases are 100,000 coins for Gold, 250,000 for Diamond, 500,000 for Emerald, 1 million for Obsidian and 2 million for Bedrock on Floors V–VII, but valuable items add their own cost.

| Floor | Drops worth checking first | Total chest price shown by the current loot table |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| IV | Spirit Wing; Recombobulator 3000 | 2M; 6M |
| V | Livid Dagger; Shadow Assassin Chestplate; Shadow Fury | 7M; 6M; 15M |
| VI | Necromancer Lord Chestplate; Giant's Sword; Precursor Eye | 10M; 25M; 30M |
| VII | Wither Chestplate; Implosion, Shadow Warp or Wither Shield; Necron's Handle | 10M; 50M; 100M |

Keep the largest possible chest price in your purse or bank before starting the floor. The [Dungeon Reward Chest page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeon_Reward_Chest) says unclaimed chests remain with Croesus for 72 hours, up to 60 runs, but that delay is a safety valve rather than a substitute for a coin reserve.

## How does score change the loot roll?

Score changes both **which chest you can open** and **how much quality the chest receives**. According to the current [Dungeon Score](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeon_Score) and [reward chest](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Dungeon_Reward_Chest#Loot_Rolling_Process) pages:

- A rank starts at 230 score and unlocks Emerald.
- S rank starts at 269.5 score, while Obsidian requires 270 score to unlock.
- S+ starts at 300, unlocks Bedrock on Floors V–VII and multiplies initial chest quality by 1.05.
- Each death removes 2 Skill score, except the first death costs 1 point when that player has a Legendary Spirit Pet in their pets menu; each failed puzzle removes 14.
- Exploration contributes up to 60 points from cleared rooms and 40 from secrets.

The S+ multiplier matters even when your target item is not in Bedrock. Chest quality acts as a budget for repeated weighted rolls, so more quality can create more opportunities for eligible items to appear. S+ is therefore not just a different chest color.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Do not rush 300 score by ignoring your class milestone:">
Class Milestone 2 is still required to claim a chest. Score belongs to the party, while the milestone is your personal loot gate.
</Callout>

## Which drops sell, and which are dead weight?

A static “always buy” list goes stale as soon as supply or demand moves. Separate drops by exit route, then use [Skyblock.bz](https://skyblock.bz/) for Bazaar items and [SkyCofl's AH flipper](https://sky.coflnet.com/flipper) for comparable Auction House items at the chest screen.

| Drop type | Examples | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bazaar-priced, standardized items | Spirit Wing, Recombobulator 3000 and eligible enchanted books | Compare the instant-sell or sell-offer proceeds directly with the full chest cost |
| Named weapons and armor | Shadow Fury, Giant's Sword, Wither Chestplate | Compare the same clean item; stars, recombobulation and other modifiers can make the cheapest listing misleading |
| Floor VII crafting pieces | Necron's Handle, Implosion, Shadow Warp, Wither Shield | Check current demand and keep enough coins for the 50M–100M chest cost |
| Filler armor, low-tier books and essence bundles | Any reward without enough combined resale value | Treat it as dead weight in a paid chest; take a free chest or leave the run unclaimed |

The rule is mechanical: **buy only when conservative proceeds from every sellable item in the chest exceed the chest price by enough to justify sale time and fees**. An expensive named drop can be dead weight at the wrong chest price, while a plain Bazaar item can be the cleanest profit in the room.

## What does one profitable Floor VI run look like?

Suppose you enter Floor VI at Catacombs XIX, reach your class milestone, finish at 300+ score and find a **Recombobulator 3000** in the Bedrock chest. The [current Floor VI loot table](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/The_Catacombs_-_Floor_VI/Loot) prices that chest at **6,000,000 coins total**.

At 10:30 UTC on August 23, 2026, the current wiki's live Bazaar data on the [Recombobulator 3000 page](https://hypixelskyblock.minecraft.wiki/w/Recombobulator_3000) showed a **9,460,597-coin sell price**. The worked margin is therefore **9,460,597 − 6,000,000 = 3,460,597 coins before Bazaar tax**. Recheck the live number at the chest screen: if the sell price falls to the 6 million break-even point, the same named drop stops being profit.

That run also illustrates why the highest-rarity chest is not always the answer. If another unlocked chest contains an item with a larger post-fee margin, buy that chest instead; normally you can claim only one chest per run unless you use a Dungeon Chest Key.

## When is a carry worth more than running your own party?

A carry has two different values. A **completion carry** buys progression and access to the next floor; a **loot carry** is a paid money run. Do not judge both with one price.

For a loot carry, use this ceiling:

> Maximum carry fee = conservative post-fee sale proceeds − chest cost − your required profit buffer

If that result is zero or negative, the carry is worth zero as a money method even if the chest contains a famous item. Running your own party also gives you practice, Catacombs XP and repeated loot access without paying a fee each run, so it becomes the better long-term choice as soon as your completion rate and score are stable.

A one-time completion carry can still be rational when it unlocks gear or the next floor you actually plan to use. It does not bypass the floor's Catacombs-level requirement, and you should not count a possible rare chest as repayment for the carry.

## Where does dungeon profit go wrong?

- **You count gross value instead of net proceeds.** Chest cost, market fees and the carry fee all come out first.
- **You move up at the unlock level.** Minimum Catacombs level says you may enter, not that your party can finish quickly or reach S+.
- **You open the famous item automatically.** A named weapon can still cost more to claim than you can receive for it.
- **You miss Class Milestone 2.** A strong party score cannot rescue your personal chest access.
- **You reroll without pricing the Kismet Feather.** A reroll is another expense, not free extra loot.
- **You quote one lucky run as an hourly rate.** Use a run log large enough to include ordinary and failed runs.

## When should you stop using this method?

Stop or move down a floor when your ten-run log shows that failures, missed S+ ranks or slow sales make net coins per minute worse than your previous floor. Rebuild the comparison whenever Hypixel changes chest weights, quality, item costs or floor mechanics, and whenever a large supply change moves the Bazaar or Auction House price of your target drops.
